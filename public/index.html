About us

A club built for
brave beginners.

Pearl Gavel Club is a student chapter in the Toastmasters tradition — a place where nervous first-timers and seasoned speakers share the same stage.

Every meeting runs like a real programme: a Toastmaster of the Day, prepared speeches, impromptu Table Topics, and constructive evaluations. You don't just learn to speak. You learn to listen, lead, and lift others up.

No experience needed. No judgement given. Just show up, and grow.

What we do

Four ways you'll grow.

01

Prepared Speeches

Craft a message, structure it, and deliver it to a room that's rooting for you.

02

Table Topics

Think on your feet. Two minutes of impromptu speaking builds real composure.

03

Evaluations

Give and receive feedback that's specific, kind, and genuinely useful.

04

Leadership Roles

Run the meeting as Toastmaster, Timer, Grammarian, or General Evaluator.
