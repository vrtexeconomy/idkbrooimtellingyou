Admissions

How to join the
Pearl Gavel Club.

We are always excited to welcome new voices. Here is everything you need to know about becoming a Gavelier.

Eligibility

Who can join?

Podar Pearl School

You must be a currently enrolled student at Podar Pearl School, located in Al Meshaf. Our club is exclusive to the school community.

Grade 6 & Above

Students must be in Grade 6 or higher to join. We focus on middle and high school students ready to take on leadership and public speaking challenges.

The Process

How to apply

Membership opens once a year. Here is the step-by-step process.

01

Watch for the Circular

Every year around September to October, the school sends out an official circular announcing club registrations. Keep an eye on your school communication apps or emails.

02

Submit the Consent Form

Attached to the circular will be a consent form. Fill this out with your parent or guardian and submit it back to the school before the deadline.

03

Welcome Aboard!

Once your form is processed, you'll be officially added to the roster. You'll receive your login credentials (Admission Number & Default Password) to access the member portal right here on this website.
